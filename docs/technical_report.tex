\title{Matched-Filter Noise Recovery and a Twelve-Member Sound-Event Ensemble for Indic Noise Event Detection}

\author{\IEEEauthorblockN{Saifuddin}
\IEEEauthorblockA{Team syehsyoh (independent participant); Senior Digital Transformation (AI \& Automation) Expert\\
Code, heads and submissions: \url{https://github.com/syehsyoh-dokling/NoiseDetection}}}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Datathon@IndoML 2026 Track~1 asks for the onset and offset of every noise event in Vaani field recordings, scored
by an event-based F1 (both boundaries within 20\,\% of the reference duration, at least 50\,ms) plus a per-clip
segment Dice. Our final submission (Codabench 994586) placed 5th of 82 leaderboard entries with 1.600895 (F1
0.780264, Dice 0.820631; maximum 2.0). The synthetic clips appear to be clean speech with noise pasted from a
finite library; mixture minus the released clean reference recovers the pasted waveforms, and a normalised
cross-correlation (NCC) matched filter over 1,198 such templates finds library waveforms in the test clips. Clips
without a library hit go to a frame-level sound-event detector (SED): a weighted mean of twelve BiGRU-head members
over frozen or fine-tuned Whisper, AST, BEATs and Dasheng encoders, with per-event peak-relative boundaries.
Training adds the gated Vaani Gold/Silver tiers and, for one member, pseudo-labels on unlabelled test audio.
\end{abstract}

\section{Training Data and Preprocessing}
\textbf{Vaani data.} The validation release holds 2,501 clips (3.51\,h, 16\,kHz mono; 1,806 natural, 695 synthetic
with clean references; 28 states, 141 state/district pairs, 27 languages) with 5,157 events in seven categories
(human non-speech 1,924; vehicle/traffic 1,281; animal 1,142; baby/child 496; phone/signal/alarm 188;
singing/music 68; appliance/machine 58). The test set holds 5,517 unlabelled clips (10.81\,h); no test labels were
released or accessed.

\textbf{Additional data (allowed by the rules~\cite{comp}).} Gold tier of the Vaani noise-event
dataset~\cite{vaanids,vaani} (10,772 clips, 19,414 verified-timestamp events; CC\,BY\,4.0, gated) for every member
except wsA and wlP; Silver tier (unverified timestamps) for wlHS (14,529 clips, HF release) and bFS (first 12,000
clips of the Kaggle copy ayanokoji2028/vaani-noise-hf). By Vaani file stem (a unique clip id), Gold shares no clip
with test or validation and no recording session (file-name prefix) with test; 13 Gold clips (0.12\,\%) share a
session with a validation clip (effect not measured). Silver extractions exclude validation and test names (not
checked by session); speaker/district overlap with test was not checked.

\textbf{Test-side information.} (a)~The leaderboard score of each upload was the final selection criterion;
Codabench~\cite{comp} lists 47 of our uploads (46 scored; 995069, after the final scored upload, failed) and the
first (921278, 10 Sep) is no longer listed, so $\ge$48 were made (quota 5/day, 100 total). (b)~Member wlP was
trained on pseudo-labels~\cite{lee} that three validation-only Whisper heads (small, medium, large, weighted
1/1/2) produced on the 3,353 test clips without a library hit: transductive use of unlabelled test audio (the
organisers' novelty direction names unlabelled Vaani audio, not test clips); the fold-$f$ student only sees
labels from teachers that never saw fold $f$.

\textbf{Preprocessing and augmentation pipeline.} Audio is read at 16\,kHz mono; no waveform augmentation or
noise mixing is used. Frozen members: encoder features are extracted once and cached (float16); per batch a random
crop of 400 or 600 frames (Table~\ref{tab:members}), then feature-level SpecAugment-style masking~\cite{specaug}:
2 time masks of width $\le$25 frames set to zero and 10\,\% of feature channels dropped (rescaled by 1/0.9).
Fine-tuned members: a random 10.24\,s crop, then on the encoder's log-mel filterbank 2 time masks ($\le$40 frames)
and 2 frequency masks ($\le$16 bins). Silver and pseudo-labelled clips enter with sample weights 0.4 and 0.5.

\textbf{Template library.} Outside the pasted spans ($\pm$150\,ms) the residual (mixture minus clean reference) of
a synthetic clip is zero at most samples (median of per-clip medians 0, maximum $4.4\!\times\!10^{-3}$ at full
scale 1), so each of the 2,213 labelled synthetic events cut from the residual is the pasted noise up to that
residual (none under the 50\,ms minimum). Templates with the same tag and length ($\pm$2 samples) and NCC $>$0.995
were merged, leaving 1,198, each with its source clips for leave-one-clip-out (LOO) evaluation.

\textbf{Features and validation.} On a 20\,ms grid, tokens of three or four layers per encoder are averaged over
frequency (AST, BEATs, Dasheng), layer-normalised per frame, averaged over layers and interpolated onto the grid
(Whisper frames used directly). Targets are eight binary channels (any noise + seven categories); 24\,\% of
natural events overlap an event of another category. 5-fold cross-validation grouped by state/district
(GroupKFold; 500--501 clips, 27--29 districts per fold) mimics unseen test districts; every validation number is
out-of-fold (OOF) with the test decoding, LOO library and cross-fitted null levels (two halves of the natural clips
calibrate each other). Decode and matcher parameters were fitted on these OOF predictions (no nested CV); each
member has one seed and seed variance was not measured.

\section{Proposed Method}
\textbf{Background.} CRNN frame classifiers~\cite{cakir,mesaros} are a standard approach to SED, scored with event-
and segment-based metrics~\cite{sedmetrics}; semi-supervised SED is a DCASE task~\cite{dcase}; Whisper keeps
background-sound identity~\cite{whisperat}. The synthetic half is known-signal detection, as in
fingerprinting~\cite{wang}. The matched filter is SNR-optimal only in white Gaussian noise~\cite{turin}; here the
interference is coloured, non-stationary speech and we do not prewhiten, so we use it as a normalised similarity
score~\cite{lewis}, not as an optimal detector.

\textbf{Pretrained models (all public, roles in Table~\ref{tab:members}).} openai/whisper-small (HF rev.
973afd2) and openai/whisper-large-v3 (06f233f)~\cite{whisper}, Apache\,2.0; MIT/ast-finetuned-audioset-10-10-0.4593
(f826b80)~\cite{ast}, BSD\,3; BEATs\_iter3\_plus\_AS2M.pt from microsoft/unilm~\cite{beats}, MIT;
mispeech/dasheng-base~\cite{dasheng}, Apache\,2.0 (rebuild used e0b0a27, last modified 25 Oct 2026, after our
training). Revisions are those used at the 28 Oct rebuild (September revisions not recorded); whisper-medium
served only as a pseudo-label teacher; CLAP and CED~\cite{ced} were only tried.

\textbf{Matched filter.}\label{sec:tpl} For a test clip and template $T_i$ the matcher computes the
energy-normalised cross-correlation (cosine, no mean removal) $c_i(s)$ at every sample lag $s$ with FFTs on the
GPU, keeping the top 2 peaks per template and clip (0.3\,s apart, NCC\,$>$\,0.2). A per-template null level
$\nu_i$ is the 0.999 quantile of $\max_s c_i(s)$ over the natural validation clips (unstored values count as 0.2);
with about 900 clips per cross-fitted half this is close to the maximum, a coarse estimate. Candidates with margin
$c_i(s)-\nu_i\ge0.1$ are selected greedily by margin with at most 30\,\% mutual overlap. In LOO mode the matcher
alone scores F1 0.9819 / Dice 0.9723 on the 695 synthetic clips; it fires on 687 of them and on 73 natural clips.

\textbf{SED members and loss.}\label{sec:sed} Each head is LayerNorm $\rightarrow$ linear (to 384) $\rightarrow$
GELU $\rightarrow$ two-layer BiGRU (hidden 192, dropout 0.3) $\rightarrow$ linear to 8 logits $z_{t,c}$. With
frame mask $m_t$, sample weight $u$ (1, Silver 0.4, pseudo 0.5) and channel weights $\lambda_0=1$,
$\lambda_{1..7}=0.5$, the loss is
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L}=\frac{\sum_{b,t} u_b m_{b,t}\sum_c \lambda_c\,\mathrm{BCE}(z_{b,t,c},y_{b,t,c})}{\sum_{b,t}u_b m_{b,t}}.
\end{equation}
Training uses AdamW~\cite{adamw} (wd $10^{-2}$), a one-cycle schedule (peak $10^{-3}$; ft encoders
$2\!\times\!10^{-5}$, fp16 autocast, gradient checkpointing), last epoch kept. A member's test probability is the
mean of its 5 fold heads (bFS: one model); the ensemble is $p=\sum_m w_m p_m/\sum_m w_m$. \emph{Weights:} the
eight v14 weights are a recipe recovered from a log (the search that produced them was not preserved); each later
member entered at one value from a ladder (aFG 2--12, bH 2--16, bFG 4--8 or swap, dH 4--12, bFS 4--16;
\texttt{models/fusion\_*}), chosen by OOF checks and/or uploads (bFG: pooled OOF gain with $\ge$4/5 folds up); not
all choices are recorded and the weights were never re-tuned jointly, so the mix is not an OOF optimum.

\textbf{Decoding and fusion.} Natural clips use two branches, $p_h=\min(p_{\text{hns}},p_0)$ for human non-speech
and $p_o=\min(\max_{c\ne\text{hns}}p_c,p_0)$ for the rest ($p_0$: any-noise channel), each decoded
peak-relatively: a region where the median-filtered curve (15 / 7 frames) clears 0.2 is kept if its peak reaches
0.34 / 0.30, and both boundaries go where the curve crosses $\alpha$ times that event's own peak ($\alpha=0.6$ /
0.5; +0.029 natural OOF F1 over one global threshold when introduced). Events under 0.1\,s are dropped, gaps below
0.1\,s / 2.0\,s closed, and branches merged with duplicate removal at IoU 0.5. A clip with any library hit is
treated as synthetic: template events plus any non-overlapping SED event (any-noise channel, threshold 0.7, median
3, minimum 0.1\,s, gap 0.04\,s). All values: \texttt{models/fusion\_Q1\_bFSadd4.json}. The system is offline and
non-causal.

\textbf{Implementation and compute.} Rebuild: Python 3.13.6, PyTorch 2.14.0, CUDA 12.6 (original versions not
recorded). Frozen heads, all aFG folds and bFG folds 0--2 ran on an RTX 4060 Laptop GPU (8\,GB); bFG folds 3--4 and
bFS on Kaggle GPUs (type not recorded). Retrain on Kaggle T4s (one GPU per job, log timestamps): aFG fold
61--63\,min, bFG fold 55--60\,min, bFS 90\,min with test pass (original 75\,min), about 11 T4-hours; a fine-tuned
test pass 4.4--5.5\,min; the five frozen passes $<$1.4\,h; the matcher 84.6\,min on the laptop GPU.

\textbf{Leaderboard.} Table~\ref{tab:res}: from 1.4591 to the final 1.6009 (our highest listed upload). The
changes up to v14 (more members, Gold data, split and peak-relative decodes) are not separable in our records and
give most of the gain; fine-tuning AST, dropping aA and adding BEATs and Dasheng add +0.0131; decoding changes
+0.0063 (OOF-motivated and LB-confirmed, except med 15); bFS +0.0007, not established. The gap to rank~1 (1.6173)
is 0.0164 (F1 0.0092, Dice 0.0072). Selection over $\ge$48 uploads leaves an unquantified optimistic bias. OOF and
LB differ by 0.12 (1.4787 vs 1.6002); 39\,\% of test clips have a library hit (validation: 28\,\% synthetic, 30\,\%
LOO hits). Approximating the test mix with the validation split, the per-clip Dice re-weights to
$0.608\cdot0.7105+0.392\cdot0.9943=0.822$ vs LB 0.8195, consistent with the mix driving the Dice part.

\textbf{Components and errors.} Table~\ref{tab:comp}: on synthetic clips the matcher alone gives 1.9542, the SED
alone 1.3378; on natural clips the SED 1.1409, the matcher 0.0691, the fusion +0.016 over SED. In the 73 natural
clips with a hit, 64 of 77 template events (83\,\%) match a reference event (1.3047 fused vs 0.9519 SED-only):
library waveforms also occur in natural recordings, so test hits are not all synthetic clips, and such true matches
raise the null levels (size not measured). Leaving one member out (decode fixed) lowers pooled OOF by 0.0097
(bFG), 0.0069 (dH), 0.0061 (bH), 0.0033 (wlH) (5/5 folds each) and 0.0027--0.0035 (aFG 3/5, wlP 4/5, wlH2 4/5,
wlHS 3/5), but raises it by 0.0001--0.0014 for wsA, wlH3 and aH, which carry no OOF support. wlP's paired fold
gain (mean +0.0026, $t\approx1.7$) is not significant; its LB effect was not measured. In Table~\ref{tab:cat} the
shortest category (human non-speech) has the highest recall; the other six lie at 0.215--0.300 for median
durations of 0.6--6.2\,s; the 2.0\,s gap closing (which merges nearby events), boundary ambiguity and scarcity are
candidate causes we did not separate. Also tried (\texttt{models/}, outcomes not uniformly recorded): soft-Dice
loss (refuted OOF), a stacker, CLAP and CED members, fine-tuned Whisper and Dasheng, a second AST seed, aH-free
mixes and the weight ladders.

\textbf{Reproduction.} After the final upload we lost backbones, fine-tuned checkpoints, cached test probabilities
and template candidates; frozen heads, configurations, library and scored zip survived. We retrained aFG, bFG and
bFS with the same scripts and seeds on Kaggle T4s, re-ran the matcher and recomputed the frozen members. The
retrained aFG/bFG give 1.4771 OOF (vs 1.4787). The rebuilt submission flags the identical 2,164 clips and, with
the scored file as reference, scores 1.9994 on them and 1.9727 on the other 3,353 (1.9858 overall; byte-identical
on 2,008 and 298 clips). It is not bit-identical (three members retrained; different GPUs, software and Dasheng
revision; causes not separated); bFS (no OOF) rests on this agreement. wlP's pseudo-labels and teacher heads are
not released, so wlP can be run but not retrained.

\textbf{Conclusion.} Known-signal detection on the synthetic half and per-event SED boundary placement on the
natural half gave 5th place. The matcher needs the test set to reuse the validation library; the ensemble is
large, offline, non-causal and not jointly optimised; wlP is transductive; natural F1 (0.45) is the weakest part.
Code, configurations, all member heads and retrained checkpoints are released (wlP's pseudo-labels and two teacher
heads excepted).
